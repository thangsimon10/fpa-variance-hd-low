# FY2026 variance memo: The Home Depot and Lowe's

**To:** FP&A leadership · **Status:** H1 actual (Q1–Q2), H2 forecast (Q3–Q4) · **Units:** $ millions unless stated

## Bottom line
Both companies are on track or slightly short on revenue, and **both are behind on margin**. Home Depot's revenue outlook is on budget ($170.4B), but its operating income is **$0.57B under** because operating costs grew faster than sales. Lowe's is **$1.36B under** on revenue and $0.43B under on operating income. That shortfall is close to the $1.0B cut Lowe's itself made to its outlook on 19 Aug 2026.

## Largest favourable changes
- **HD Q2 revenue +$1.00B (+2.1%).** This is mostly phasing, not outperformance. The budget follows FY2025's quarterly shape, and GMS only joined on 4 Sep 2025, so FY2025's first half had no GMS revenue. Comparable sales were +1.7%. The H1 beat (+$1.51B) reverses in H2 (Q3 −$0.57B, Q4 −$0.96B).
- **LOW Q1 revenue +$0.52B (+2.3%).** Same mechanism. FBM closed on 9 Oct 2025, so it is in FY2026 Q1 but absent from the base quarter. Underlying demand is soft: Q2 comparable sales were +0.2%.

## Largest unfavourable changes
- **LOW Q4 revenue −$1.60B (−7.2%), the largest variance in the model.** Given H1 actuals, reaching the $93.0B budget needs +6.2% H2 growth. With FBM already in the FY2025 Q4 base and comparable-sales guidance cut to flat, the forecast H2 growth is +2.9%.
- **HD H1 operating expenses −$0.62B (3.5% over budget).** Opex grew 7.0% year over year against 5.3% revenue growth, which is consistent with carrying GMS's cost base. It absorbed the whole gross-profit beat.
- **LOW Q2 gross margin 33.0% vs 33.8% budget (−$0.15B).** A lower-margin pro distribution mix is plausible but not separately disclosed. Carried into H2, it takes full-year gross profit $1.11B under budget.

## Recommended actions
1. **Rebase the H2 budget for Lowe's to its lowered $92.0B outlook** so H2 variances measure performance, not an outdated target.
2. **Hold Home Depot H2 SG&A growth at or below revenue growth.** At the current run-rate its operating margin lands at 12.2%, below the 12.4–12.6% guidance.
3. **Phase the FY2027 budget on a pro forma basis**, with the acquired businesses in every quarter of the prior-year base. This removes the timing noise that made H1 look like a beat.

## Method, briefly
- **Budget.** Company FY2026 guidance midpoints (24/25 Feb 2026), phased by FY2025 quarter shares. Margins keep FY2025 seasonality, shifted to land on the guided full year. Lowe's does not guide gross margin, so it is held at the FY2025 actual (33.5%).
- **Forecast.** Q3–Q4 = FY2025 same quarter × (1 + comparable-sales guidance midpoint), plus the Q3 stub of the acquisition that was missing from the base. Margins carry the H1 gap to budget. This is conservative: part of that gap is acquisition mix that the H2 budget already contains.
- **Grain.** Quarterly. Public filings do not report months, so monthly splits would be invented.
